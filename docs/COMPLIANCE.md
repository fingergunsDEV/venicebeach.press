# Commercial operations and launch review

Status: technical safeguards implemented; **legal compliance is not certified and launch review remains open**. Owner confirmed on October 16, 2026: **Holistic Growth Marketing LLC**, a California single-member LLC. VeniceBeach.buzz and VeniceBeach.press are commercial websites under VeniceBeach.media. Initial revenue comes from custom advertisement placements for artists/influencers and businesses. Google ads, behavioral advertising, online checkout, and automatic billing are not implemented.

## Implemented controls

- Both footers identify the legal operator and commercial purpose. The privacy notice identifies the California company; the parent's separately published privacy policy is linked without claiming it covers identical practices.
- Paid cards identify advertising and the advertiser. Paid articles have a prominent disclosure before the headline, and paid outbound links use `rel=sponsored`. This is not a substitute for clear disclosure within screenshots, social posts, emails, video/audio, or third-party distribution.
- Independent editorial and paid production are distinct. No guarantees of praise, sales, search rank, audience size, or revenue. No invented testimonials or published performance figures.
- Inquiries/submissions acknowledge a notice at collection and do not opt into marketing. Adult eligibility and submission review authority are recorded with notice version and timestamp. Publication, likeness, music, photography, and promotional reuse still need documented permissions.
- Newsletter consent expressly names the publisher/network, cultural updates and sponsor promotions. Consent version/time and later withdrawal time are recorded. Live provider requests are blocked without public contact/address configuration and explicit operator confirmation of email setup. Double opt-in is used; no confirmation is fabricated.
- Signup responses offer a private subscription-management link. Withdrawal is a POST action requiring the secret token, with no login or fee. Local suppression is recorded first. If provider synchronization fails, an administrator-only privacy follow-up is created and the response clearly states that provider unsubscribe is unconfirmed. The operator must resolve it; local suppression alone cannot stop Mailchimp sends.
- Privacy requests have their own form, no marketing checkbox, and an administrator-only queue. Editor and commercial roles cannot access private privacy requests. Authentication, CSRF controls for staff actions, form validation, rate limits, restrictive CSP, and secure-cookie settings reduce technical risks.
- No Google scripts, behavioral-advertising pixels, affiliate program, ad cookies, or third-party embeds. Aggregate application counters have no visitor identifiers. An essential staff sign-in cookie is disclosed. Do not add a cookie banner that falsely implies a tracking system or consent manager is already configured.
- Recording a commercial agreement requires a signed-agreement reference and confirmation of core terms. This records the operator's assertion, not a signature or legal validation. The sites do not charge cards, create subscriptions, calculate taxes, or send invoices.

## Open launch prerequisites — operator and qualified counsel

1. **Public identity/contact:** confirm the legal name against registration, rights to brands/domains, any DBA/fictitious-name requirements, and publisher authority. Configure a monitored `PRIVACY_EMAIL` and a valid public `BUSINESS_ADDRESS`. The owner supplied 215 Market Street, Venice, CA 90291; site privacy/accessibility contacts info@venicebeach.buzz and info@venicebeach.press; publisher contact jason@holisticgrowthmarketing.com; and network contact info@venicebeach.media. These public defaults are now stored in src/business.js; independent mailbox delivery and address verification have not been performed. Never invent a contact email or expose a private home address without the owner's intent.
2. **Parent privacy consistency:** owner supplied https://holisticgrowthmarketing.com/privacy.html. The network proxy returned 403 when attempting to read it. Its contents and consistency with the two site notices remain unverified. Reconcile actual controller identity, contact channels, processors, retention, transfers, and rights before launch. Do not simply replace the site-specific notice with a policy describing different data practices.
3. **California/general privacy:** evaluate CalOPPA and other applicable privacy requirements. Determine CCPA/CPRA applicability using current thresholds, business relationships, actual processing, and jurisdiction, rather than assuming either applicability or exemption from a $10K MRR goal. Identify processors, hosting/network logs, vendors, contracts, transfers, rights/verification/appeals, any required authorized-agent process, and legal response deadlines. Access requests should not require a purchase or marketing permission. Monitor privacy requests daily and triage separately from sales. Do not solicit IDs in public forms.
4. **Retention and rights fulfillment:** approve concrete retention schedules per record type, legal holds, and backup expiry; the current app does not automatically purge data. On a verified request, locate leads, subscriber/consent/suppression records, related published material, email-provider records, and backups. Verify identity proportionately, disclose only the requester's data, correct/delete as authorized, document exceptions, and preserve only lawfully necessary suppression/audit evidence. Confirm provider removal and backup aging. The queue records requests; it does not automatically fulfill legal rights.
5. **Email:** verify sender/domain authentication, accurate sender/subject, identification of commercial mail where required, physical mailing address, a working accessible unsubscribe route, confirmation/suppression behavior, provider agreement, and legally required opt-out processing times. Do not transfer inquiry contacts into a marketing list. Mailchimp confirmation, campaign templates, and provider-hosted unsubscribe must be tested in a real account. Persist and resolve failed unsubscribe syncs before sending further campaigns. `EMAIL_COMPLIANCE_CONFIRMED=yes` is an operator assertion, not proof or a legal waiver.
6. **Advertising/influencers:** obtain substantiation for objective claims; verify sponsor identity, offer restrictions, and rights. Label placements before users engage and at every distribution point. Disclose gifts, affiliate compensation and other material connections even if no money changes hands. Commercial content must not masquerade as independent reviews. Review campaigns involving minors, alcohol, health claims, contests, political ads, or other regulated categories separately before accepting them. No regulated-ad category is implicitly approved by this software.
7. **Campaign contracts:** use a counsel-reviewed insertion order/services agreement naming Holistic Growth Marketing LLC and the advertiser. Cover authority, inventory, placements, channels, assets/rights, sponsor disclosure, deliverables, dates/timezone, approvals/revision limits, pricing/taxes, payment, cancellation/refunds, missed-delivery remedies, measurement limits, privacy, liability, termination, and applicable dispute terms. Do not treat generic website terms or an inquiry checkbox as the commercial contract. Have a California accountant review entity, income/sales-tax, local license, and reporting obligations as applicable; no tax exemption is assumed.
8. **Recurring offers:** current price cards are quotes, not checkout. Before adding automatic renewals or payments, review then-current California automatic-renewal and other applicable rules, consent, disclosures, reminders, proof of authorization, cancellation ease, refund policy, payment-provider scope, PCI handling, and tax treatment. Never take raw card data through existing forms. Recurring agreements recorded in the internal dashboard do not authorize charges.
9. **IP/copyright:** secure licenses, contributor agreements, credits, likeness/publicity releases, and permissions for each medium/use. Maintain a copyright complaint process. Assess whether to register and publish a designated DMCA agent before claiming safe-harbor protections; no registration is represented. Obtain appropriate permissions for creator interviews and any minors before publication.
10. **Accessibility/security:** retain WCAG 2.2 AA target; perform keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, and actual-content checks beyond axe. Provide direct support email. Configure HTTPS, persistent storage, encrypted backups, least privilege, individual authenticated access, incident response, dependency maintenance, and staff procedures. Test email forms, public policies, and rights workflows on both domains.
11. **Google ads later:** adding AdSense or other ad tech is a new processing decision. Review Google's then-current publisher/ad policies, invalid-traffic controls, required privacy disclosures, regional certified-CMP/consent requirements where applicable, California sale/sharing opt-out and GPC handling if applicable, children's treatment, vendor lists, and cross-domain behavior. Implement and test consent/opt-outs before any ad or tracker runs. No AdSense approval, certification, or compliant configuration is claimed now.
